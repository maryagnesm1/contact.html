Milestone 5 includes the website contact page, which allows visitors to submit a questions using a form. The page uses a split-screen layout, with an AI-generated video on the left and the contact form on the right. The video is based on Johannes Vermeer's oil painting A Lady Writing a Letter. The source of the image is the National Gallery of Art’s Creative Commons collection. I used Meta AI to transform the image into a short video using prompts to add more interest and interaction for the viewer. I chose this artwork because it depicts a woman writing a letter to an unknown recipient. The theme is communication, and I wanted to connect the art history theme with how technology has changed our methods of communication.
